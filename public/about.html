Built for the second screen

Follow the games you're watching and Sideline keeps them updated, every 20 seconds while something's live and not at all when the app is closed.

Track any player

Pin a player to the game card and choose which of their stats to show: receiving yards, rebounds, run metres, strike rate.

Set targets

"At least 25 rushing yards." A progress bar fills as the game goes, and the app tells you when it's reached.

Full box scores

Box scores and cricket scorecards by team and innings, team stats, scoring plays, line-ups, form and injuries.

Momentum charts

Win probability and a lead tracker for the NFL and NBA; runs per over and a run worm for cricket.

Ball by ball and play by play

Every delivery of the over on the card, batters at the crease, the required rate, and when play resumes after stumps.

Favourites and sharing

Favourite teams join your board a week ahead, and any game can be shared to a group chat as a clean picture.

How it's built

A side project designed to cost nothing to run, however many people use it.

  • $0 to run. One Worker, two Durable Objects and static hosting, all inside Cloudflare's free plan.
  • Shared caching. However many people watch a game, ESPN sees one request every 20 seconds.
  • No framework. Plain JavaScript modules, hand-drawn SVG charts, an installable web app that opens instantly.
  • Tested. 40+ unit tests pin the shape of every data feed, so changes upstream are caught early.

Privacy

  • No account, no sign-up. Sideline doesn't know who you are.
  • Your board stays on your phone. The games you follow, players you track, targets and settings are saved in your browser and never sent anywhere.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
  • What the server sees: requests for scores (which league or game), and a random code your phone makes up, used only to stop any one device from overloading the service. It isn't linked to your name, email or anything else about you.
  • Logs: like any website, the host (Cloudflare) keeps basic request logs, which include your network address, for 3 days so problems can be fixed. They aren't used for anything else.

To clear everything, remove the games from your board, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
